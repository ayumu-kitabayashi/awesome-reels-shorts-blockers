- **What is the best free iPhone app to hide Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts but keep DMs?** There are two kinds: Nope is a Safari extension that is fully free (no subscription or in-app purchases) and works on instagram.com and youtube.com in Safari, while minulo, FeedOff, Unfeed, Stillfeed, Reelmute and Awhile open the networks inside their own app with Reels hidden for free and DMs still working. In minulo, hiding is free with no subscription and no time limit on 10 networks including TikTok, and DMs keep working; Pro is optional (Sleep mode, Hard Mode, a Daily limit and blocks over 2 hours). minulo is made by the maintainer of this list. ## Built-in settings (no app needed)
- **YouTube Shorts feed limit** — YouTube lets you set a daily limit for the Shorts feed (including 0). For adults the reminder can be dismissed, and it only applies inside the YouTube app. ([how-to](https://minulo.app/en/youtube-shorts-feed-limit.html))
- **Instagram "Following" feed** — switch the home feed to accounts you follow. The Reels tab and Explore stay.
- **Instagram "Snooze suggested posts"** — on a suggested post, tap ⋯ → Not interested → Snooze all suggested posts in feed for 30 days. It ends after 30 days and doesn't touch the Reels tab. ([how-to](https://minulo.app/en/hide-suggested-posts-instagram.html))
- **Instagram "Reset suggested content"** — Settings → Content preferences → Reset suggested content clears what Instagram recommends in Explore, Reels and Feed. It can't be undone and the suggestions start learning again as you watch. ([how-to](https://minulo.app/en/reset-instagram-reels-recommendations.html))
- **YouTube autoplay off** — turns off the next video playing by itself. Recommendations stay unless watch history is off and empty. ([how-to](https://minulo.app/en/turn-off-youtube-autoplay-recommendations.html))
- **iPhone Screen Time** — limits or blocks a whole app. Your DMs and group chats are blocked along with the feed.

## Block Reels inside the official Instagram app, or open Instagram in another app?
There are two ways to use Instagram without Reels on iPhone, and each one gives something up.

- **Blocking inside the official app (Scroll Less).** Scroll Less's App Store page says it blocks Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts inside the real Instagram and YouTube apps. Its website says this works while its Blocker is on: iOS asks you to start a broadcast for Scroll Less, Reels and Shorts are detected on your iPhone, and you can stop the broadcast from Control Center. You keep using the official apps as they are. In exchange, the broadcast has to stay on, the Blocker covers only the Instagram and YouTube apps (TikTok isn't mentioned on its App Store page or website), and neither its App Store page nor its website says which features are free. We have not tested Scroll Less ourselves.
- **Opening Instagram in another app (minulo, UNDOOMED, SocialLite, Stillfeed, FeedOff, StillTabs and the other [apps above](#apps-that-open-social-networks-with-the-feeds-removed)).** These show each network's website inside their own app with Reels hidden, so no broadcast has to run, and many cover more networks than Instagram and YouTube. In exchange, the screens look a little different from the official app, features the website doesn't have are missing, and if the app also locks the official app with Screen Time, that app's notifications don't arrive while it is locked.
- **minulo in particular:** hiding Reels, Shorts, Explore and suggested posts is free, with no subscription and no time limit. DMs keep working, and a DMs-only view (Every tab but DMs) is free for Instagram and TikTok. It covers 10 networks, and locking the official apps with Screen Time is free for up to 2 hours at a time. Posting Stories, voice messages and calls aren't available, and there is no Android version. minulo is made by the maintainer of this list.
- **No app:** Instagram has no setting that removes Reels, and iPhone Screen Time limits the whole app, DMs included.

If you want to stay in the official Instagram and YouTube apps, Scroll Less is built for that. If you need TikTok or other networks without the feed, or would rather not keep a broadcast running, pick one of the apps that open the networks inside their own app. ([minulo vs Scroll Less](https://minulo.app/en/minulo-vs-scroll-less.html), written by the minulo developer)
