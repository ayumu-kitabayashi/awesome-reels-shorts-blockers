Facts come only from each app's US App Store page or official website (checked October 2026; ratings counts last updated 2026-10-24). Ratings counts change daily. Corrections and additions are welcome via issues or pull requests.

## Built-in settings (no app needed)
- **YouTube Shorts feed limit** — YouTube lets you set a daily limit for the Shorts feed (including 0). For adults the reminder can be dismissed, and it only applies inside the YouTube app. ([how-to](https://minulo.app/en/youtube-shorts-feed-limit.html))
- **Instagram "Following" feed** — switch the home feed to accounts you follow. The Reels tab and Explore stay.
- **Instagram "Snooze suggested posts"** — on a suggested post, tap ⋯ → Not interested → Snooze all suggested posts in feed for 30 days. It ends after 30 days and doesn't touch the Reels tab. ([how-to](https://minulo.app/en/hide-suggested-posts-instagram.html))
- **Instagram "Reset suggested content"** — Settings → Content preferences → Reset suggested content clears what Instagram recommends in Explore, Reels and Feed. It can't be undone and the suggestions start learning again as you watch. ([how-to](https://minulo.app/en/reset-instagram-reels-recommendations.html))
- **YouTube autoplay off** — turns off the next video playing by itself. Recommendations stay unless watch history is off and empty. ([how-to](https://minulo.app/en/turn-off-youtube-autoplay-recommendations.html))
- **iPhone Screen Time** — limits or blocks a whole app. Your DMs and group chats are blocked along with the feed.

## Whole-app blockers (block DMs too)
Useful if you want to stop using an app entirely; they don't remove Reels while keeping messages.
- **[Opal](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id1497465230)** — blocks chosen apps on a schedule with Screen Time.
- **[one sec](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id1532875441)** — adds a breathing pause before an app opens.
- **[ScreenZen](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id1541027222)** — adds a wait before opening apps; supported by tips.
- **[No Scroll](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6474079216)** — you choose a time limit before opening an app; when it runs out, the app is locked for 5 minutes.
- **[DMOnly](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6773196876)** — keeps chosen social apps locked and lets you open them in 15-minute windows.
- **[Curfew](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6755434607)** — locks chosen apps (such as Instagram or YouTube) on a schedule with Screen Time; unlocking starts a 5-minute wait. One schedule is free; Pro is a one-time purchase.
- **[숏패스 (ShortPass)](https://apps.apple.com/kr/app/id6803353215)** (Korea) — locks chosen apps during study hours or after a daily limit, with an exam D-day; locking is in Pro (non-renewing passes).

## How to choose
- **Need Instagram only for DMs and group chats?** Look for a DMs-only / Messages Only mode (UNDOOMED, LoomWeb, Konvo, minulo, Reelmute, Stillfeed).
- **Need TikTok or Snapchat too?** Check the networks column — not every app supports them.
- **Keep opening the official app out of habit?** Pick an app that locks the official app with Screen Time (SocialLite, minulo, noShorts, snowscroll, Reelmute, Konvo).
- **Want everything free?** Nope, ShortformBlock and Still are free Safari extensions; noShorts, Dome, Noreel and Close say they are free; several in-app browsers make basic hiding free.
