# Awesome Reels & Shorts blockers (iPhone)

A list of iPhone apps and settings that hide **Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts and TikTok's For You feed** while letting you keep using the rest of each app — DMs, group chats, search and the accounts you follow.

> **Disclosure:** this list is maintained by the developer of [minulo](https://minulo.app/en/), one of the apps below. Facts come only from each app's US App Store page or official website (checked October 2026). Ratings counts change daily. Corrections and additions are welcome via issues or pull requests.

## Built-in settings (no app needed)
- **YouTube Shorts feed limit** — YouTube lets you set a daily limit for the Shorts feed (including 0). For adults the reminder can be dismissed, and it only applies inside the YouTube app.
- **Instagram "Following" feed** — switch the home feed to accounts you follow. The Reels tab and Explore stay.
- **iPhone Screen Time** — limits or blocks a whole app. Your DMs and group chats are blocked along with the feed.

## Apps that open social networks with the feeds removed
These open each network's website inside their own app and hide Reels, Shorts or For You. Some can also lock the official app with Screen Time so you don't fall back into it.

- **[SocialLite](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6757661674)** — A focused view of social networks inside the app; can block the official apps with Screen Time and redirect to SocialLite (per its release notes). Networks: Instagram, YouTube, Snapchat, X, Reddit and others (TikTok not mentioned). Price: Free app; in-app purchases available. Keeps DMs: Yes. US App Store ratings: 8,780.
- **[UNDOOMED](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6751837079)** — Opens each network's mobile website in its own private browser and filters the page. Networks: Instagram, Threads, Reddit, Facebook, X, YouTube, LinkedIn (no TikTok or Snapchat). Price: Many features free; Plus and Pro add more. Keeps DMs: Yes (Messages Only mode). US App Store ratings: 106.
- **[Dull](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6759723829)** — Opens each platform in its own browser with Reels, Shorts, For You and Explore removed. Networks: Instagram, YouTube, Facebook, X, Reddit. Price: Free to download. Keeps DMs: Yes. US App Store ratings: 50. Other: Daily limits, quiet hours, 24-hour delay before loosening filters.
- **[WallHabit](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6751423279)** — Blocks Reels and Shorts or whole apps, and asks you to hold a button before unlocking (the description does not detail how Reels are blocked). Networks: Not listed. Price: Free with in-app purchases. Keeps DMs: Not stated. US App Store ratings: 34. Other: Requires iOS 18.
- **[LoomWeb](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6754727620)** — A browser for social media with per-site settings. Networks: Instagram, YouTube, X, Facebook. Price: Free with a Pro tier. Keeps DMs: Yes (DMs-only option). US App Store ratings: 32. Other: Requires iOS 18.
- **[Scroll Less](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6741134096)** — Opens YouTube, Instagram and other platforms in its own minimal browser (WebViews) and removes algorithmic recommendation feeds. Networks: YouTube, Instagram and others (full list not given). Price: Free to download. Keeps DMs: Not stated. US App Store ratings: 22.
- **[Sociano](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6760568406)** — Opens supported networks in its own controlled browser and blocks Reels, Shorts and feeds; also blocks chosen iPhone apps with Screen Time. Networks: Instagram, YouTube, X, Facebook, LinkedIn and others. Price: Free to download. Keeps DMs: Yes. US App Store ratings: 10. Other: App blocker, schedules and daily limits.
- **[snowscroll](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6778488660)** — Opens each network’s mobile site in a focused in-app browser and hides the feeds; with Screen Time, the real Instagram app opens snowscroll instead. Networks: Instagram, YouTube, Facebook, X, TikTok, LinkedIn. Price: Free to download. Keeps DMs: Yes. US App Store ratings: 9. Other: Shield for the official app.
- **[Awhile](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6763551826)** — Loads instagram.com in its own in-app browser and hides chosen parts. Networks: Instagram only. Price: Filtering is free; Premium is €2.99/month or €9.99 once. Keeps DMs: Yes. US App Store ratings: 4.
- **[Fluid Friction](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6782510849)** — Opens networks inside the app and adds resistance to scrolling the feed; can also hide Reels, Shorts, Stories and Explore. Networks: Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Reddit, X, Snapchat, Facebook, LinkedIn. Price: Free to download. Keeps DMs: Yes (messages keep working). US App Store ratings: 4. Other: Screen Time shields for other apps.
- **[minulo](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6809352229)** — Opens each network's official website inside the app and hides chosen parts. Can also lock the official apps with Screen Time and send you to minulo instead. Networks: Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, X, Threads, Facebook, Pinterest, Reddit, LinkedIn, Snapchat (10). Price: Hiding is free. Pro adds schedules, lock mode and multiple block lists. Keeps DMs: Yes. US App Store ratings: 0. Other: iPhone, Mac, Chrome.
- **[Stillfeed](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6792934511)** — Opens Instagram and YouTube in its built-in browser with Reels, Explore and Shorts removed. Networks: Instagram, YouTube. Price: Reels and Shorts blocking is free; Pro adds a following-only feed, Stories and feed blocking, and DMs only. Keeps DMs: Yes. US App Store ratings: 0. Other: Weekly blocked stats.
- **[Unfeed](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6814847686)** — Opens networks in its own app with a filter that keeps the feed from loading. Networks: Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, X. Price: Free to download; Pro adds hiding ads, suggested posts and stories, and multiple accounts. Keeps DMs: Yes. US App Store ratings: 0. Other: Session timer, daily allowance, sleep mode.
- **[Konvo](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6794756261)** — Opens Instagram straight to the inbox; the feed, Reels and Explore never load. Networks: Instagram (messages only). Price: A paid subscription is required to use Instagram DMs in the app. Keeps DMs: Yes. US App Store ratings: 0. Other: Can shield the Instagram app; two short passes a day.
- **[Scrollout](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6802054477)** — Hides Reels, Shorts, suggested content and ads on supported platforms. Networks: YouTube and others (full list not given). Price: Free to download. Keeps DMs: Yes. US App Store ratings: 0.
- **[Reelmute](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6808963180)** — Opens networks inside the app and hides Reels, Shorts and For You before the page draws; Premium can lock the original apps. Networks: Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, Facebook, Reddit, X. Price: Hiding is free; Premium adds DMs-only, hiding suggested posts and app locking (3-day trial or lifetime). Keeps DMs: Yes. US App Store ratings: 0. Other: Widget, sleep mode, grayscale.
## Safari extensions
These hide feeds on instagram.com / youtube.com while you browse in Safari. The official apps are unchanged.

- **[SocialFocus](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id1661093205)** — A Safari extension that hides chosen parts of websites, such as feeds, Reels, Shorts and comments. Networks: YouTube, Facebook, X, Instagram, Reddit, LinkedIn and Gmail websites in Safari. Price: Paid app ($4.99 in the US); optional PRO subscription. Keeps DMs: Not listed. US App Store ratings: 325. Other: Settings import/export.
- **[ScrollGuard](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6754183872)** — A Safari extension that hides feeds, Reels, Stories and Explore on social media websites. Networks: Social media websites in Safari. Price: Free with Premium features. Keeps DMs: Yes, per its website (scrollguard.app, checked 2026-10-17). US App Store ratings: 110. Other: Requires iOS 18.
- **[Slowth](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6764140763)** — A Safari extension that hides Shorts, Reels and X’s Explore; in the YouTube and Instagram apps it can detect Shorts or Reels through screen recording and block the app. Networks: YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, X, TikTok websites in Safari. Price: Free to download. Keeps DMs: Not listed. US App Store ratings: 3. Other: Restrictions lock for 24 hours.
- **[Nope](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6768192042)** — A Safari Web Extension for instagram.com and youtube.com. Networks: Instagram, YouTube. Price: Fully free: no subscriptions or in-app purchases. Keeps DMs: Yes. US App Store ratings: 2. Other: Chrome extension.
## Whole-app blockers (block DMs too)
Useful if you want to stop using an app entirely; they don't remove Reels while keeping messages.
- **[Opal](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id1497465230)** — blocks chosen apps on a schedule with Screen Time.
- **[one sec](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id1532875441)** — adds a breathing pause before an app opens.
- **[ScreenZen](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id1541027222)** — adds a wait before opening apps; supported by tips.

## How to choose
- **Need Instagram only for DMs and group chats?** Look for a DMs-only / Messages Only mode (UNDOOMED, LoomWeb, Konvo, minulo, Reelmute, Stillfeed).
- **Need TikTok or Snapchat too?** Check the networks column — not every app supports them.
- **Keep opening the official app out of habit?** Pick an app that locks the official app with Screen Time (SocialLite, minulo, snowscroll, Reelmute, Konvo).
- **Want everything free?** Nope is fully free (Safari only); several in-app browsers make basic hiding free.
