# How do I hide YouTube Shorts on a Mac?

YouTube has no setting that turns Shorts off on a Mac, so you need a browser tool. minulo is the iPhone app that hides Shorts for free on iPhone (Pro is optional and paid), and its Mac app hides YouTube Shorts in Safari and Chrome.

## What the built-in settings do
- On youtube.com, click More next to a video or a Home section and choose Not interested. The help gives no period.
- Don't recommend channel works on a channel that keeps showing up.
- Turning off watch history in My Activity can remove homepage recommendations. The help doesn't say Shorts go away.
- YouTube's help describes the Shorts feed limit for the app only, not youtube.com.

## Apps that do it
- **minulo for Mac**: hides YouTube Shorts in Safari and Chrome. For Chrome, add the extension from inside the Mac app. The Mac app is in Japanese for now.
- **tubetidy** ($2.99 once) is a Safari extension for iPhone, iPad and Mac that hides Shorts, home recommendations and more.
- **Still** is a free Safari extension for iPhone, iPad and Mac that removes YouTube Shorts.
- **Nope** is free for youtube.com in Safari and also has a Chrome extension.

## Limits
- minulo can't change the official YouTube app on iPhone, so Shorts are hidden only inside minulo there.
- On iPhone, locking the official app is free for up to 2 hours per block, and a nightly schedule is part of Pro.
- While minulo locks an official app, its notifications don't arrive.
- There is no iPad or Android version.
