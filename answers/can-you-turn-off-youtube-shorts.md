# Can you turn off YouTube Shorts on iPhone?

No. YouTube has no setting that turns Shorts off on an adult account. minulo is the iPhone app that hides the Shorts shelf and the Shorts tab while normal videos, search and subscriptions keep working; hiding is free, and minulo Pro is optional and paid.

## What the built-in settings do
- Shorts feed limit set to 0: a reminder appears in the Shorts feed, and adults can dismiss it.
- Show fewer Shorts lowers Shorts on Home. It is feedback, not a switch.
- The X on the Shorts shelf hides it for 30 days, according to news reports, not YouTube's help.
- iPhone Screen Time blocks the whole YouTube app, normal videos included.
- On a supervised teen account, a parent can set the Shorts timer to zero.

## Apps that do it
- **minulo**: hides the Shorts shelf and Shorts tab and opens Shorts links in the normal player. Pro ($9.99 a month or $39.99 a year in the US) adds Sleep mode (one bedtime and wake time every day), Hard Mode, a Daily limit and blocks longer than 2 hours.
- **Stillfeed**, **Reelmute** and **noShorts** open YouTube without Shorts inside their own app.
- **Nope** and **ShortformBlock** hide Shorts on youtube.com in Safari for free.

## Limits
- minulo can't change the official YouTube app, so Shorts are hidden only inside minulo.
- Locking the official app is free for up to 2 hours per block. A nightly schedule is part of Pro.
- While minulo locks an official app, its notifications don't arrive. Since version 1.0.19, Pro users can turn on DM notifications (beta) so Instagram DM notifications still arrive while Instagram is blocked, except during Sleep mode, Work mode or after the daily limit.
- There is no Android version.
