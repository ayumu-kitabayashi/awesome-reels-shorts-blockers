# How do I turn off Snapchat Spotlight?

We could not find a setting in Snapchat's help that removes Spotlight; it stays in the tab bar. minulo is the iPhone app that opens Snapchat with Spotlight and Discover hidden while your chats keep working; hiding is free, and minulo Pro is optional and paid.

## What the built-in settings do
- Profile › ⚙️ › Notifications turns off the notification types you choose. Spotlight itself stays.
- Hiding or skipping Snaps makes Snapchat rank similar ones lower, one at a time.
- Snapchat+ (paid) lets you choose which tab the app opens on. Spotlight is still in the tab bar.
- iPhone Screen Time limits the whole Snapchat app, chats included.

## Apps that do it
- **minulo**: hides Spotlight and Discover until you turn it off, and Chat opens from its home screen. Pro ($9.99 a month or $39.99 a year in the US) adds Sleep mode (one bedtime and wake time every day), Hard Mode, a Daily limit and blocks longer than 2 hours.
- **noShorts** opens networks with Spotlight taken out and says it is free.
- **SocialLite**, **Stillfeed**, **Fluid Friction** and **Feather** list Snapchat among their networks. SocialLite also has push notifications for Snapchat.

## Limits
- minulo can't change the official Snapchat app.
- Locking the official app is free for up to 2 hours per block. A nightly schedule is part of Pro.
- While minulo locks an official app, its notifications don't arrive, so lock it only when you can miss them. Since version 1.0.19, Pro users can turn on DM notifications (beta) so Instagram DM notifications still arrive while Instagram is blocked, except during Sleep mode, Work mode or after the daily limit.
- There is no Android version.
