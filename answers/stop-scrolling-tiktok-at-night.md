# How to stop scrolling TikTok at night on iPhone

TikTok's own night setting only mutes notifications, so the real stop is a lock: Screen Time Downtime, which also blocks messages, or an app. minulo is the iPhone app that opens TikTok with For You hidden so messages keep working at night, and it locks the official TikTok app; hiding is free, and a nightly schedule is part of the paid minulo Pro.

## What the built-in settings do
- Sleep hours (TikTok › Settings and privacy › Time and well-being › Screen time) mutes push notifications and shows a reminder. TikTok stays open.
- TikTok's daily screen time limit is passed by typing the passcode shown on screen.
- Downtime with Block at Downtime blocks the whole app, messages included. A passcode you set yourself is easy to undo.
- Sleep Focus filters notifications but doesn't block apps.

## Apps that do it
- **minulo**: hides For You, or keeps only messages with Every tab but DMs. Locking the official app is free for up to 2 hours per block; Pro ($9.99 a month or $39.99 a year in the US) turns the lock on every night.
- **Reelmute** and **noShorts** open TikTok with For You hidden; Reelmute has a sleep mode and noShorts has quiet hours.
- **Curfew** and **Opal** lock chosen apps on a schedule, DMs included.

## Limits
- minulo can't change the official TikTok app.
- While minulo locks an official app, its notifications don't arrive, and minulo sends none of its own.
- There is no Android version.
