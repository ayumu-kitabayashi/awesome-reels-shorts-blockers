# Does YouTube Premium remove Shorts?

No. YouTube's list of Premium benefits has nothing that hides Shorts. minulo is the iPhone app that hides the Shorts shelf and the Shorts tab without Premium; hiding is free, and minulo Pro is optional and paid.

## What the built-in settings do
- Premium covers ads before and during videos, downloads, background play, picture-in-picture and more.
- YouTube's help says background play isn't available in the Shorts player, and Auto speed and 1080p Premium aren't offered for Shorts.
- We couldn't confirm in YouTube's help how Premium treats ads on Shorts.
- The Shorts feed limit can be set to 0 without Premium, but the reminder can be dismissed.

## Apps that do it
- **minulo**: hides the Shorts shelf and Shorts tab and opens Shorts links in the normal player. Pro ($9.99 a month or $39.99 a year in the US) adds Sleep mode (one bedtime and wake time every day), Hard Mode, a Daily limit and blocks longer than 2 hours.
- **Stillfeed**, **Reelmute** and **noShorts** open YouTube without Shorts inside their own app.
- **Nope** and **ShortformBlock** hide Shorts on youtube.com in Safari for free. **No Shorts: Blocker for YouTube** and **tubetidy** cost $2.99 once.

## Limits
- minulo can't change the official YouTube app, so Shorts are hidden only inside minulo.
- Locking the official app is free for up to 2 hours per block. A nightly schedule is part of Pro.
- While minulo locks an official app, its notifications don't arrive.
- There is no Android version.
