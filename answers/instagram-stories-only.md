# Can I watch only Stories on Instagram, without Reels?

Instagram has no Stories-only mode, and the Reels tab sits at the bottom of the screen the whole time. In minulo you can hide the Reels tab, Explore and suggested posts; friends' Stories stay at the top of the feed, you can still use DMs, and hiding is free.

## Steps
1. Built in: open Stories from the row at the top of your feed. To drop someone's posts but keep their Stories, open their profile, tap **Following**, then **Mute**, and turn on **Posts** only.
2. In minulo, open Instagram and log in on Instagram's own page.
3. Tap the gear next to Instagram and turn on **Reels tab**, **Explore tab** and **Suggested posts**.
4. Optional: lock the official Instagram app in the **Block** tab, free for up to 2 hours per block.

## Other options
- **Following feed**: tap Instagram at the top left and choose Following for posts from accounts you follow, newest first. Instagram doesn't remember it, so you pick it again each time.
- **Stillfeed** opens Instagram on Following. Blocking Stories and the feed is in its Pro.
- **limitd** opens Instagram with the feed, Reels, Stories and Explore hidden from the start, and needs a paid subscription to use.

## FAQ
### Can I post Stories in minulo?
No. Posting Stories isn't available, because Instagram's website doesn't have it. Voice messages and calls aren't available either.

### Will people know I muted their posts?
No. Instagram's help says it doesn't tell them, and muting is not the same as unfollowing.

### Does this change the Instagram app?
No. Reels are hidden only while you use Instagram inside minulo. The official app keeps its Reels tab.
