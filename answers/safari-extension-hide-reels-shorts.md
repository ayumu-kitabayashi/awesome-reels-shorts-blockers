# Is there a Safari extension that hides Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts on iPhone?

Yes, several, and they work only on websites you open in Safari, not in the Instagram or YouTube apps. minulo is the iPhone app (not a Safari extension) that hides Reels and Shorts inside its own app and can lock the official apps; hiding is free, and minulo Pro is optional and paid.

## What the built-in settings do
- Safari: page menu › Manage Extensions to find one, then Settings › Apps › Safari › Extensions › Allow Extension.
- Neither Instagram nor YouTube has a setting that turns off Reels or Shorts for adults.
- An extension helps most if you delete the app and use the website.

## Apps that do it
- **Nope**: instagram.com and youtube.com, fully free.
- **ScrollGuard**: free with Premium features; hides feeds, Reels, Stories and Explore.
- **#blockit**: Shorts, Reels and endless feeds on popular websites; 7-day free trial.
- **Still**: YouTube Shorts and Instagram and Facebook Reels, free.
- **SocialFocus** ($4.99), **Slowth**, **No Reel For Instagram** ($3.39), **No Shorts** ($2.99 once), **ShortformBlock** (free) and **tubetidy** ($2.99 once) cover one or more of these sites.
- **minulo**: hides Reels, Shorts and suggested posts on 10 networks inside the app. Pro ($9.99 a month or $39.99 a year in the US) adds Sleep mode (one bedtime and wake time every day), Hard Mode, a Daily limit and blocks longer than 2 hours.

## Limits
- minulo can't change the official apps either; it hides feeds only inside minulo.
- Locking the official app is free for up to 2 hours per block. A nightly schedule is part of Pro.
- While minulo locks an official app, its notifications don't arrive.
- There is no Android version.
