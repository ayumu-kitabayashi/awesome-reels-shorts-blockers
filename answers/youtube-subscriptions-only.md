# How do I watch only my subscriptions on YouTube without recommendations?

The YouTube app always opens on Home, and we could not find a setting that makes it open on Subs. minulo is the iPhone app that opens YouTube on Subscriptions with Home recommendations and Shorts hidden; hiding is free, and minulo Pro is optional and paid.

## What the built-in settings do
- Subs is a feed in the top bar of Home with the newest videos from your channels. You tap it each time.
- Pausing watch history can remove Home recommendations, but YouTube's help says the top bar, Subs included, isn't visible while history is paused.
- Not interested and Don't recommend channel trim Home; it keeps recommending.
- Show fewer Shorts gives fewer Shorts, not none.

## Apps that do it
- **minulo**: Subscriptions only hides Shorts, Home recommendations, comments, channel posts and search; videos from your subscriptions still play. Hide Shorts and recommendations keeps search. Watch history can stay on. Pro ($9.99 a month or $39.99 a year in the US) adds Sleep mode (one bedtime and wake time every day), Hard Mode, a Daily limit and blocks longer than 2 hours.
- **LockedIn** has optional switches for YouTube home recommendations and subscriptions.
- **TheSNSisMine** hides recommended posts and Shorts on YouTube's website (subscription required).
- **tubetidy** ($2.99 once) hides home recommendations, related videos and Shorts in Safari.

## Limits
- minulo can't change the official YouTube app.
- Locking the official app is free for up to 2 hours per block. A nightly schedule is part of Pro.
- While minulo locks an official app, its notifications don't arrive, and minulo sends none of its own. Since version 1.0.19, Pro users can turn on DM notifications (beta) so Instagram DM notifications still arrive while Instagram is blocked, except during Sleep mode, Work mode or after the daily limit.
- There is no Android version.
