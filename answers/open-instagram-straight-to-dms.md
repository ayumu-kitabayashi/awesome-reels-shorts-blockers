# How to open Instagram straight to DMs on iPhone without the feed

Instagram has no setting that makes the app open on your messages. minulo is the iPhone app that opens Instagram with only your DMs and the notifications tab left; its Every tab but DMs setting is free, and minulo Pro is optional and paid.

## What the built-in settings do
- Tapping a message notification opens that chat, but the feed and Reels are one tap away after it.
- We couldn't confirm whether the Instagram icon's Quick Actions menu currently has a messages item.
- You can turn off every notification type except messages, one by one, so the alerts you tap are DMs.
- iPhone Screen Time limits the whole app, DMs included.

## Apps that do it
- **minulo**: Every tab but DMs hides Reels, Explore, the home feed and suggested posts; DMs and group chats stay. Pro ($9.99 a month or $39.99 a year in the US) adds Sleep mode (one bedtime and wake time every day), Hard Mode, a Daily limit and blocks longer than 2 hours.
- **Konvo** opens Instagram straight to the inbox; a paid subscription is required for DMs.
- **UNDOOMED** (Messages Only mode), **LoomWeb** (DMs-only option), **Stillfeed** (DMs Only in Pro) and **Reelmute** (DMs-only in Premium) also have a messages view.

## Limits
- minulo can't change the official Instagram app.
- Locking the official app is free for up to 2 hours per block. A nightly schedule is part of Pro.
- While minulo locks an official app, its notifications don't arrive. Since version 1.0.19, Pro users can turn on DM notifications (beta) so Instagram DM notifications still arrive while Instagram is blocked, except during Sleep mode, Work mode or after the daily limit.
- Posting Stories, voice messages and calls aren't available.
- There is no Android version.
