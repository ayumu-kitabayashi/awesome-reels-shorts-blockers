# If I hide the Shorts shelf on YouTube Home, how long does it stay hidden?

About 30 days, according to news reports, though YouTube's own help doesn't describe the X or give a period. minulo is the iPhone app that keeps the Shorts shelf and the Shorts tab hidden until you change the setting; hiding is free, and minulo Pro is optional and paid.

## What the built-in settings do
- SlashGear and GeekChamp quote the message "Shelf will be hidden for 30 days".
- The X isn't on every account. tbreak (April 2026) found it only on the mobile website.
- Hiding the shelf leaves Shorts in the Shorts tab, search, Subscriptions and channel pages.
- Show fewer Shorts is feedback with no period, and you can undo it in My Activity.

## Apps that do it
- **minulo**: hides the Shorts shelf and Shorts tab with no 30-day reset. Pro ($9.99 a month or $39.99 a year in the US) adds Sleep mode (one bedtime and wake time every day), Hard Mode, a Daily limit and blocks longer than 2 hours.
- **Stillfeed**, **Reelmute** and **noShorts** open YouTube without Shorts inside their own app.
- **Nope** and **ShortformBlock** hide Shorts on youtube.com in Safari for free; **tubetidy** costs $2.99 once.

## Limits
- minulo can't change the official YouTube app, so the shelf is hidden only inside minulo.
- Locking the official app is free for up to 2 hours per block. A nightly schedule is part of Pro.
- While minulo locks an official app, its notifications don't arrive. Since version 1.0.19, Pro users can turn on DM notifications (beta) so Instagram DM notifications still arrive while Instagram is blocked, except during Sleep mode, Work mode or after the daily limit.
- There is no Android version.
