# Can you turn off Reels on Instagram?

No. We could not find any setting in Instagram's help that turns off Reels for an adult account. minulo is the iPhone app that opens Instagram with the Reels tab and Reels in the feed hidden while your DMs keep working; hiding is free, and minulo Pro is optional and paid.

## What the built-in settings do
- The Following feed shows accounts you follow in time order, without suggested posts. The Reels tab stays.
- The daily time limit is a reminder for all of Instagram, not a block.
- Sleep mode mutes notifications and auto-replies to DMs. It doesn't hide Reels.
- Teen Accounts get a reminder after 60 minutes and sleep mode at night, but Reels stay.
- iPhone Screen Time can only block the whole app, DMs included.

## Apps that do it
- **minulo**: hides the Reels tab, Reels in the feed and Explore, and can lock the official app so tapping it sends you to minulo. Pro ($9.99 a month or $39.99 a year in the US) adds schedules, longer blocks and Hard Mode.
- **UNDOOMED**, **Awhile**, **SocialLite** and **LoomWeb** open Instagram in their own view with Reels hidden and DMs kept.
- **Stillfeed** and **Reelmute** hide Reels for free inside their own app.
- **Nope** and **ScrollGuard** hide Reels in Safari.

## Limits
- minulo can't change the official Instagram app, so Reels are hidden only inside minulo.
- Locking the official app is free for up to 2 hours per block. A nightly schedule is part of Pro.
- While minulo locks an official app, its notifications don't arrive.
- There is no Android version.
