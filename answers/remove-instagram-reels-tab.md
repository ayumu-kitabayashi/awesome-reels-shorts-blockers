# Can you remove the Reels tab from Instagram on iPhone?

Not in the Instagram app: we found no setting that removes, hides or moves the Reels tab. minulo is the iPhone app that opens Instagram with the Reels tab hidden while your DMs keep working; hiding is free, and minulo Pro is optional and paid.

## What the built-in settings do
- Tapping Instagram at the top of Home and choosing Following shows posts from people you follow in time order. The Reels tab stays in the bottom bar.
- iPhone Screen Time limits whole apps or categories, not one tab, so DMs are limited too.
- In October 2025 Instagram's head said Instagram was testing Reels and DMs as the first two tabs for a small group in India.

## Apps that do it
- **minulo**: hides the Reels and Explore tabs from the bar, and Every tab but DMs leaves only DMs and notifications. Pro ($9.99 a month or $39.99 a year in the US) adds Sleep mode (one bedtime and wake time every day), Hard Mode, a Daily limit and blocks longer than 2 hours.
- **UNDOOMED**, **Awhile**, **LoomWeb**, **Stillfeed** and **Reelmute** also open Instagram with Reels hidden while DMs keep working.
- **Close** (Korea) is Instagram without the Reels and Explore tabs.
- **Nope** (free) and **No Reel For Instagram** ($3.39) hide Reels navigation on instagram.com in Safari.

## Limits
- minulo can't change the official Instagram app, so the Reels tab is still there if you open it directly.
- Locking the official app is free for up to 2 hours per block. A nightly schedule is part of Pro.
- While minulo locks an official app, its notifications don't arrive.
- There is no Android or iPad version.
