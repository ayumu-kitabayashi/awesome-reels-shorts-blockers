# How do I block Instagram and TikTok during work hours on weekdays but still use DMs?

iPhone Screen Time can block apps during set hours with an App Limit or a Downtime schedule, but it blocks DMs along with the feed, and a Work Focus only quiets notifications. minulo's Work mode (Pro) locks the apps you pick on the days and hours you choose, and the block screen's **Open in minulo** button opens them with Reels and For You hidden, where you can still use DMs.

## Steps
1. Built in: in Settings, Focus, set up **Work**, allow only work apps to notify you, choose a Home Screen page without social apps and have it turn on during your work hours.
2. In minulo, add Instagram and TikTok and hide Reels and For You from the gear next to each. This is free.
3. With Pro, turn on **Work mode**, pick Instagram and TikTok, and set your workdays and hours.
4. Without Pro, lock the apps in the **Block** tab, free for up to 2 hours per block, and start the next block yourself.

## Other options
- **Screen Time App Limit** with Block at End of Limit, or **Downtime** with Block at Downtime (Screen Time Schedule on iOS 27). These are free, but DMs are blocked too, and anyone who knows the passcode can tap past them.
- **Work Focus** keeps social apps quiet, but you can still open them.
- Put the phone in a bag or drawer during focused work and keep work messages on the computer.

## FAQ
### Will I get Instagram DM notifications during work hours?
On iPhone, while minulo locks an official app, its notifications don't arrive. The Pro DM notifications beta (since 1.0.19) doesn't work during Work mode, so if you rely on Instagram notifications for work, leave the official app unlocked and use minulo for browsing. On Android, notifications from a blocked app still arrive.

### Can I still post for work?
Yes. Inside minulo, posting, comments and DMs work. Stories, voice messages and calls need the official app.

### Does minulo change the official apps?
No. Reels and For You are hidden only inside minulo. minulo works on iPhone and Android, and there's a Mac app too.
