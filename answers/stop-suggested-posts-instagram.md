# How do I stop "Suggested for you" posts in my Instagram feed?

You can only pause them: Instagram's longest option snoozes suggested posts in your Feed for 30 days, and we found no setting that turns them off for good. minulo is the iPhone app that keeps suggested posts hidden every time you open Instagram while your DMs keep working; hiding is free, and minulo Pro is optional and paid.

## What the built-in settings do
- Options (⋯) on a suggested post › Not interested › Snooze pauses all suggested posts in your Feed for 30 days. Reels and Explore keep recommending.
- Not interested also lets you stop posts from one account or with certain words.
- The Following feed (tap the Instagram logo) has no suggested posts, but you pick it each time.
- Reset suggested content clears recommendations, but suggestions come back as you use the app.

## Apps that do it
- **minulo**: hides suggested posts, and Reels, Explore and Stories one switch at a time. Pro ($9.99 a month or $39.99 a year in the US) adds Sleep mode (one bedtime and wake time every day), Hard Mode, a Daily limit and blocks longer than 2 hours.
- **Reelmute** hides suggested posts in Premium. **Unfeed** hides suggested posts in Pro.
- **Stillfeed** opens Instagram on Following for free.
- **Scrollout** hides suggested content. **No Reel For Instagram** hides suggested accounts in Safari.

## Limits
- minulo can't change the official Instagram app.
- Locking the official app is free for up to 2 hours per block. A nightly schedule is part of Pro.
- While minulo locks an official app, its notifications don't arrive. Since version 1.0.19, Pro users can turn on DM notifications (beta) so Instagram DM notifications still arrive while Instagram is blocked, except during Sleep mode, Work mode or after the daily limit.
- There is no Android version.
