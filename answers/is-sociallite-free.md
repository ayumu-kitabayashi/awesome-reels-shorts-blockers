# Is SocialLite free?

SocialLite is free to download with in-app purchases, and its description doesn't say which features are free. minulo is the iPhone app that hides Reels and Shorts without buying anything; hiding is free, and minulo Pro is optional and paid.

## What the built-in settings do
This question is about price, so here is what SocialLite charges on the US App Store (checked 2026-10-26):
- Pro: $6.99 to $17.99 a month, or $19.99 to $47.90 a year, depending on the offer shown.
- Family: $12.99 a month.
- SocialLite has push notifications for Instagram and Snapchat, and 16,883 US ratings at 4.7 stars (checked 2026-11-04).

## Apps that do it
- **minulo**: hiding Reels, Shorts and suggested posts on 10 networks and the Every tab but DMs setting are free. Pro is $9.99 a month or $39.99 a year in the US, or a one-time lifetime purchase.
- **Stillfeed** and **Reelmute** hide Reels and Shorts for free; DMs-only views are paid.
- **Awhile** filters Instagram for free; Premium is $2.99 or $14.99.
- **UNDOOMED** has many free features; Plus is $1.99 a month and Pro has a $29.99 lifetime option.
- **Nope** is fully free but works only in Safari on instagram.com and youtube.com.

## Limits
- minulo can't change the official apps, so feeds are hidden only inside minulo.
- Locking the official apps is free for up to 2 hours per block. A nightly schedule is part of Pro.
- While minulo locks an official app, its notifications don't arrive, and minulo has no push notifications of its own.
- There is no Android version.
