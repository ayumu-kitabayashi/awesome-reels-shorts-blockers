# How do I get rid of the For You tab on X?

You can't remove it: we could not find a setting in X's help that does, and Home always has both tabs. minulo is the iPhone app that opens X on Following with the For you tab hidden while your messages keep working; hiding is free, and minulo Pro is optional and paid.

## What the built-in settings do
- Tap Following at the top of Home. X reopens on whichever timeline you had open when you closed it, so close it on Following.
- If you look at For you even once before closing, X opens there next time.
- Muted words keep chosen words, hashtags or @names out of Home and notifications. Muted posts still show in search.
- iPhone Screen Time limits or blocks the whole X app, messages included.

## Apps that do it
- **minulo**: Open Following first hides the For you tab, and Hide Search and Hide Video and Grok are there too. Messages, notifications and Following open from its home screen. Pro ($9.99 a month or $39.99 a year in the US) adds Sleep mode (one bedtime and wake time every day), Hard Mode, a Daily limit and blocks longer than 2 hours.
- **Reelmute**, **Dull** and **Feather** list X among their networks and hide For You.
- **UNDOOMED**, **LoomWeb** and **Stillfeed** also cover X.
- **Slowth** hides X's Explore in Safari.

## Limits
- minulo can't change the official X app.
- Locking the official app is free for up to 2 hours per block. A nightly schedule is part of Pro.
- While minulo locks an official app, its notifications don't arrive. Since version 1.0.19, Pro users can turn on DM notifications (beta) so Instagram DM notifications still arrive while Instagram is blocked, except during Sleep mode, Work mode or after the daily limit.
- There is no Android version.
