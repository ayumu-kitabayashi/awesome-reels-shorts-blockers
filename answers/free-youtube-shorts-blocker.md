# Free way to block YouTube Shorts on iPhone but keep normal videos

YouTube's own free options either can be dismissed or block the whole app, so the free way that keeps normal videos is to watch YouTube in an app that hides Shorts. minulo is the iPhone app that hides the Shorts shelf and the Shorts tab while normal videos, search and subscriptions keep working; hiding is free, and minulo Pro is optional and paid.

## What the built-in settings do
- Shorts feed limit set to 0 is free, but an adult account can dismiss the reminder.
- Show fewer Shorts is free feedback, not a switch.
- iPhone Screen Time is free and strict, but it blocks the whole YouTube app, including the videos you wanted.

## Apps that do it
- **minulo**: hides the Shorts shelf and Shorts tab and opens Shorts links in the normal player. Hiding stays on until you change it. Pro ($9.99 a month or $39.99 a year in the US) adds Sleep mode (one bedtime and wake time every day), Hard Mode, a Daily limit and blocks longer than 2 hours.
- **Stillfeed** and **Reelmute** hide Shorts for free in their own app.
- **noShorts** says it is free with no subscription or in-app purchases.
- **Nope** and **ShortformBlock** are free Safari extensions for youtube.com.

## Limits
- minulo can't change the official YouTube app, so Shorts are hidden only inside minulo.
- Locking the official app is free for up to 2 hours per block. A nightly schedule is part of Pro.
- While minulo locks an official app, its notifications don't arrive. Since version 1.0.19, Pro users can turn on DM notifications (beta) so Instagram DM notifications still arrive while Instagram is blocked, except during Sleep mode, Work mode or after the daily limit.
- There is no Android version, and no iPad version.
