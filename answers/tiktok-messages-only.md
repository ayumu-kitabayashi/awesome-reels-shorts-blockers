# How can I use TikTok only for messages?

TikTok has no messages-only setting; the app opens straight onto For You. minulo is the iPhone app that opens TikTok with For You and the other tabs hidden while your messages keep working; hiding is free, and minulo Pro is optional and paid.

## What the built-in settings do
- TikTok has a Following tab, but the app still opens on For You.
- TikTok's daily screen time limit is passed by typing a passcode shown on screen.
- iPhone Screen Time can limit TikTok, but that blocks your messages too.

## Apps that do it
- **minulo**: Every tab but DMs hides For You and the other tabs and leaves messages. Instagram has the same setting. It can also lock the official TikTok app so its icon shows an Open in minulo button. Pro ($9.99 a month or $39.99 a year in the US) adds Sleep mode (one bedtime and wake time every day), Hard Mode, a Daily limit and blocks longer than 2 hours.
- **Reelmute**, **noShorts** and **Feather** open TikTok with For You hidden.
- **Stillfeed**, **snowscroll**, **Unfeed** and **Fluid Friction** list TikTok among their networks.

## Limits
- minulo can't change the official TikTok app, so For You is hidden only inside minulo.
- Locking the official app is free for up to 2 hours per block. A nightly schedule is part of Pro.
- While minulo locks an official app, its notifications don't arrive, and minulo sends none of its own. Since version 1.0.19, Pro users can turn on DM notifications (beta) so Instagram DM notifications still arrive while Instagram is blocked, except during Sleep mode, Work mode or after the daily limit.
- There is no Android version.
