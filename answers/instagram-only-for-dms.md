# How do I use Instagram only for DMs on iPhone?

Instagram has no messages-only mode, and iPhone Screen Time limits the whole app, so your DMs get blocked along with Reels. In minulo, the free **Every tab but DMs** setting hides Reels, Explore, the home feed and suggested posts, and leaves only DMs (group chats included) and the notifications tab.

## Steps
1. Optional, built in: in the Instagram app go to your profile, Menu, then Notifications, and turn off each type that isn't about messages.
2. Install minulo, open Instagram inside it and log in on Instagram's own login page. minulo doesn't receive or store your password.
3. Tap the gear next to Instagram and turn on **Every tab but DMs**.
4. Optional: lock the official Instagram app in the **Block** tab, free for up to 2 hours per block. Tapping the icon then shows a block screen, and **Open in minulo** takes you to your DMs.

## Other options

- **Konvo** opens Instagram straight to the inbox, but needs a paid subscription to use DMs.

## FAQ
### Do group chats work?
Yes. Group chats are part of Instagram DMs, so you can read and reply to them in minulo's DMs-only view.

### Will I get message notifications?
Notifications come from the official Instagram app. If you leave it unlocked, they arrive as usual. While minulo locks it, none arrive. Pro users can turn on DM notifications (beta) so Instagram DM notifications still arrive while Instagram is blocked, except during Sleep mode, Focus time in Work mode or after the daily limit.

### Does minulo change the Instagram app itself?
No. The DMs-only view exists only inside minulo. The official app still opens on the regular feed unless you lock it.
