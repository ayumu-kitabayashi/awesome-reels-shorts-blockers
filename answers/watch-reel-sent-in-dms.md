# How to watch a Reel a friend sent in DMs without getting pulled into Reels

In the Instagram app you can't: a Reel opened from a chat plays in the Reels viewer, and one swipe carries on into the feed. minulo is the iPhone app that stops a Reel sent in DMs before the Reels feed starts; hiding Reels is free, and minulo Pro is optional and paid.

## What the built-in settings do
- Instagram has no setting that hides the Reels tab on adult accounts.
- A Screen Time limit on Instagram blocks the chat along with Reels.
- The Following feed changes Home only; the Reels tab stays.

## Apps that do it
- **minulo**: with the Reels and Explore tabs switch on, a Reel opened from a DM pauses before the feed. Open for 5 min lets you watch, and blocking turns back on by itself afterwards. Shorts sent in DMs pause the same way. Pro ($9.99 a month or $39.99 a year in the US) adds Sleep mode (one bedtime and wake time every day), Hard Mode, a Daily limit and blocks longer than 2 hours.
- **LockedIn** has an optional switch for DM video playback.
- **Konvo** opens Instagram straight to the inbox, and the feed, Reels and Explore never load; DMs need a paid subscription.

## Limits
- minulo can't change the official Instagram app, so a Reel opened there still leads into the feed.
- Locking the official app is free for up to 2 hours per block. A nightly schedule is part of Pro.
- While minulo locks an official app, its notifications don't arrive.
- There is no Android version.
