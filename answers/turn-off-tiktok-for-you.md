# How do I turn off the TikTok For You page?

We could not find a setting in TikTok's help that turns off For You; TikTok's tools change what it shows but don't remove it. minulo is the iPhone app that opens TikTok with For You hidden while your messages keep working; hiding is free, and minulo Pro is optional and paid.

## What the built-in settings do
- Refresh your For You feed starts recommendations over. It can't be undone, and the feed fills up again.
- Not interested, Filter keywords (up to 100) and Manage topics remove some posts.
- Following and Friends are there, but TikTok still opens on For You.
- In some regions, such as the EU, turning off personalization makes For You a Popular feed.
- iPhone Screen Time limits the whole app, messages included.

## Apps that do it
- **minulo**: Recommendations hides For You; Every tab but DMs keeps only messages. Messages and Following open from its home screen. Pro ($9.99 a month or $39.99 a year in the US) adds schedules, longer blocks and Hard Mode.
- **Reelmute**, **noShorts** and **Feather** open TikTok with For You hidden.
- **Slowth** and **Still** cover the TikTok website in Safari; Still blocks it.

## Limits
- minulo can't change the official TikTok app.
- Locking the official app is free for up to 2 hours per block. A nightly schedule is part of Pro.
- While minulo locks an official app, its notifications don't arrive.
- There is no Android version.
