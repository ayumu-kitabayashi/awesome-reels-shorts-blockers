# How to use Instagram DMs without opening the app

You can use DMs on instagram.com in a browser, but the website has Reels and Explore too, so a browser alone doesn't make Instagram DMs only. minulo is the iPhone app that opens Instagram's website with only your DMs and the notifications tab left; that setting is free, and minulo Pro is optional and paid.

## What the built-in settings do
- On a computer, open instagram.com and click Messages on the left. Reels and Explore are a click away.
- On iPhone, Settings › Notifications › Instagram › Show Previews can show a DM alert without opening the app. Whether the message text appears is up to Instagram.

## Apps that do it
- **minulo**: on iPhone, Every tab but DMs hides Reels, Explore, the home feed and suggested posts. On a Mac, its Mac app hides the Reels and Explore tabs and the stories tray on instagram.com in Safari and Chrome while Messages keeps working (the Mac app is in Japanese for now). Pro ($9.99 a month or $39.99 a year in the US) adds Sleep mode (one bedtime and wake time every day), Hard Mode, a Daily limit and blocks longer than 2 hours.
- **Konvo** opens Instagram straight to the inbox; a paid subscription is required for DMs.
- **UNDOOMED**, **LoomWeb**, **Stillfeed** and **Reelmute** have a messages view; in Stillfeed and Reelmute it is paid.

## Limits
- minulo can't change the official Instagram app.
- Locking the official app is free for up to 2 hours per block. A nightly schedule is part of Pro.
- While minulo locks an official app, its notifications don't arrive. Since version 1.0.19, Pro users can turn on DM notifications (beta) so Instagram DM notifications still arrive while Instagram is blocked, except during Sleep mode, Work mode or after the daily limit.
- There is no Android version.
