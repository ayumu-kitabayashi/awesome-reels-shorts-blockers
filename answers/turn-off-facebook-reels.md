# How do I turn off Reels on Facebook?

You can't with a setting: we found none in Facebook's help, and since September 2025 every video on Facebook is a reel. minulo is the iPhone app that opens Facebook with Reels and Watch hidden while messages keep working; hiding is free, and minulo Pro is optional and paid.

## What the built-in settings do
- On a reel, More › Not Interested shows fewer similar reels. Facebook says the effect builds slowly and fades.
- More › Hide reel removes that reel and reduces content from its creator, one reel at a time.
- Settings › Media › Never Autoplay Videos stops videos from playing by themselves. Reels still appear.
- iPhone Screen Time blocks the whole Facebook app.

## Apps that do it
- **minulo**: Hide Reels, Watch and Marketplace and Hide suggested posts; Notifications, Messages, Friends and Feed open from its home screen. Pro ($9.99 a month or $39.99 a year in the US) adds Sleep mode (one bedtime and wake time every day), Hard Mode, a Daily limit and blocks longer than 2 hours.
- **Yava** blocks Facebook Reels and Watch while letting you message friends (subscription required).
- **UNDOOMED**, **Dull**, **Reelmute** and **noShorts** cover Facebook among their networks.
- **Still** removes Facebook Reels in Safari for free.

## Limits
- minulo can't change the official Facebook app, so Reels are hidden only inside minulo.
- Locking the official app is free for up to 2 hours per block. A nightly schedule is part of Pro.
- While minulo locks an official app, its notifications don't arrive. Since version 1.0.19, Pro users can turn on DM notifications (beta) so Instagram DM notifications still arrive while Instagram is blocked, except during Sleep mode, Work mode or after the daily limit.
- There is no Android or iPad version.
