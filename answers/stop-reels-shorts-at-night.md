# How do I stop scrolling Reels and Shorts at night on iPhone?

iPhone Screen Time Downtime with **Block at Downtime** locks whole apps at night, but it blocks Instagram DMs too. minulo hides only Reels and Shorts for free inside its own app, and it can lock the official Instagram and YouTube apps when you get into bed, so you can still message friends.

## Steps
1. Built in: go to Settings, Screen Time, Downtime, choose Scheduled and set times such as 11 PM to 7 AM. Set a Screen Time passcode and turn on **Block at Downtime**. On iOS 27 this is called Screen Time Schedule.
2. To keep DMs, add minulo to **Always Allowed** (Settings, Screen Time, Always Allowed).
3. In minulo, add Instagram and YouTube and hide Reels and Shorts from the gear next to each. This is free.
4. Or skip Downtime: lock the official apps in minulo's **Block** tab when you go to bed, free for up to 2 hours per block. Sleep mode in Pro does this every night from your bedtime to your wake time.

## Other options
- **Sleep Focus** (Health, Browse, Sleep) filters notifications from wind down, but it doesn't block apps.
- **Grayscale** (Accessibility, Display & Text Size, Color Filters) turns the screen gray. Nothing is blocked.
- **Scroll Less** blocks Reels and Shorts inside the official Instagram and YouTube apps while its Blocker broadcast is on. Its pages don't mention a bedtime schedule.
- **Dull** removes Reels, Shorts, For You and Explore in its own browser and includes quiet hours. It needs a subscription after a 7-day free trial.

## FAQ
### Will I get message notifications at night?
On iPhone, while minulo blocks an official app, that app's notifications don't arrive. The Pro DM notifications beta (since 1.0.19) doesn't work during Sleep mode either. On Android, minulo only puts its own screen over a blocked app when you open it, so that app's notifications still arrive.

### Does minulo change the official apps?
No. Reels and Shorts are hidden only inside minulo, which is why locking the official apps at night helps.

### Does Instagram's own Sleep mode help?
It mutes Instagram notifications and auto-replies to DMs, but Reels are still there if you open the app.
