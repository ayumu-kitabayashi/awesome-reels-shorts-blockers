# What's a good SocialLite alternative for iPhone?

SocialLite's FAQ says new signups need a paid subscription and there is no universal free tier. minulo works the same basic way: it opens social networks in its own view with Reels and Shorts hidden, you can still use DMs, it can lock the official apps with Screen Time, and hiding is free.

## Steps
1. Install minulo and open Instagram inside it. You sign in on Instagram's own page, and there is no minulo account.
2. Tap the gear next to Instagram and turn on **Reels tab** and **Explore tab**, or **Every tab but DMs** if you only want messages.
3. Add YouTube and TikTok with **Add a service** to hide Shorts and For You.
4. Like SocialLite, minulo can lock the official apps: in the **Block** tab, pick them in Apple's app list. This is free for up to 2 hours per block, and the block screen has an **Open in minulo** button.

## Other options
- **UNDOOMED** opens each network's mobile site in a private browser. Many features are free, and Plus and Pro add more. It has no TikTok or Snapchat.
- **LoomWeb** is a browser for social media with per-site settings, free with a Pro tier.
- **Awhile** covers Instagram only. Filtering is free and Premium is paid.
- **ScrollGuard** works as a Safari extension and in its own app, free with Premium features. **Nope** is a fully free Safari extension for instagram.com and youtube.com.

## FAQ
### What does SocialLite do that minulo doesn't?
SocialLite has far more ratings, and it sends push notifications for Instagram and Snapchat. minulo is new and has only a few US ratings so far.

### Which networks does minulo cover?
Ten: Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, X, Threads, Facebook, Pinterest, Reddit, LinkedIn and Snapchat.

### Are there limits?
minulo can't change the official apps, so the hiding happens only inside minulo. It is iPhone only (plus a Mac version for Safari and Chrome), with no Android version.
