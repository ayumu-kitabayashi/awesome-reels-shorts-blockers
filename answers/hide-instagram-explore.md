# How do I hide the Explore page on Instagram?

Instagram has no setting that removes the Explore tab; we could not find one in its help as of October 2026. minulo is the iPhone app that opens Instagram with Explore and Reels hidden while your DMs keep working; hiding is free, and minulo Pro is optional and paid.

## What the built-in settings do
- Not interested (touch and hold a post in Explore) hides that post and shows fewer like it.
- Content preferences › Sensitive content › Less cuts back on upsetting posts.
- Reset suggested content starts recommendations over across Explore, Reels and Feed. It can't be undone.
- Your Algorithm changes topics in Reels and Explore, not the tab.
- iPhone Screen Time can't target Explore, and blocking the app blocks DMs too.

## Apps that do it
- **minulo**: the Reels and Explore tabs switch hides both. Suggested posts, Stories or every tab but DMs can be hidden too. Pro ($9.99 a month or $39.99 a year in the US) adds schedules, longer blocks and Hard Mode.
- **Dull** removes Explore (subscription required). **Dome** removes Reels, Shorts and Explore pages.
- **LockedIn** has optional switches for Search and Explore. **Close** (Korea) has no Explore tab.
- **ScrollGuard** and **No Reel For Instagram** hide Explore in Safari.

## Limits
- minulo can't change the official Instagram app, so Explore is hidden only inside minulo.
- Locking the official app is free for up to 2 hours per block. A nightly schedule is part of Pro.
- While minulo locks an official app, its notifications don't arrive.
- There is no Android version.
