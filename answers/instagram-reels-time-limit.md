# How do I set a time limit for Instagram Reels only?

You can't: we could not find a Reels-only time limit in Instagram's help or in iPhone Screen Time. minulo is the iPhone app that hides Reels instead of timing them while your DMs keep working; hiding is free, and minulo Pro is optional and paid.

## What the built-in settings do
- Instagram's daily time limit counts all of Instagram and only shows a reminder.
- Screen Time App Limits work on the whole app up to iOS 26, and by category such as Social Media on iOS 27. DMs are blocked too.
- Limits can be ignored by default. A Screen Time passcode with Block at End of Limit makes them stick.
- The Following feed removes suggested posts from Home, but the Reels tab stays.

## Apps that do it
- **minulo**: hides Reels, Explore and suggested posts. A Reel sent in a DM pauses before the feed, and Open for 5 min lets you watch for five minutes. Pro ($9.99 a month or $39.99 a year in the US) adds Sleep mode (one bedtime and wake time every day), Hard Mode, a Daily limit and blocks longer than 2 hours.
- **SocialLite**, **UNDOOMED**, **Stillfeed** and **Reelmute** hide Reels while DMs keep working.
- Whole-app timers such as **No Scroll** lock the app when your chosen time runs out, DMs included.

## Limits
- minulo can't change the official Instagram app.
- Locking the official app is free for up to 2 hours per block. A nightly schedule is part of Pro.
- While minulo locks an official app, its notifications don't arrive.
- minulo launched in September 2026 and has few ratings. There is no Android version.
